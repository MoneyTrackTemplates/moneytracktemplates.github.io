Free Means Free

The goal is simple: ordinary classified advertisements can be posted free, businesses can create a basic listing free, and visitors can search the directory free.

No subscription required just to look for an advertisement or find a business.

🛡️ A Free Directory Still Needs Rules

Before public posting opens, MoneyTrackTemplates will establish posting rules, prohibited-item policies, reporting tools, advertiser verification and moderation procedures.

Advertisers will be responsible for having the right to use the text, photographs and other materials they submit or import.

The directory is being designed to connect people with advertisements and businesses. MoneyTrackTemplates does not currently process purchases or payments between directory users.
