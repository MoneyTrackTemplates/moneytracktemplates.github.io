Introducing an original monthly series

Diana’s Country Almanac

A little wisdom for every season—delivered one month at a time.

Read September 2026

There is something wonderful about an old-fashioned almanac.

It is not quite a calendar, not quite a cookbook, not quite a gardening guide, and not quite a book of stories.

It is all of those things.

For generations, almanacs have helped people keep track of the seasons, watch the skies, plan their gardens, preserve food, manage their households, and simply pay a little more attention to the world around them.

They were useful. They were entertaining. And sometimes, they were delightfully peculiar.

✦ ✦ ✦

We thought it was time to bring that spirit back.

Welcome to a new kind of almanac

One month at a time.

Diana’s Country Almanac is an original 2026–2027 series for today’s American household, created with the charm and personality of an old-fashioned country almanac.

Rather than asking readers to wait for one enormous book, we are releasing it in monthly installments. Each edition will bring seasonal information, recipes, regional gardening advice, nature, astronomy, household ideas, folklore, humor, and—because we could not resist—horoscopes for all twelve signs of the zodiac.

Read each edition online, print it, save it, and eventually collect all twelve installments into your own annual almanac.

A nod to the great serial tradition

Something new to look forward to.

Long before people binge-watched television shows or waited for the next podcast episode, readers often encountered favorite stories one installment at a time. Charles Dickens was one of the great masters of serialized publishing. His readers had to wait for the next installment, creating something that has become surprisingly rare in modern life: anticipation.

We are borrowing that delightful idea—not Dickens’s stories, of course, but the pleasure of having something new to look forward to. September will be different from October. Winter will bring its own recipes and traditions. Spring will bring gardening and renewal. Summer will bring harvests, preserving, outdoor living, and long evenings beneath the stars.

An almanac for all fifty states

Seasonal wisdom should respect where people live.

A gardener in Maine does not experience September like a gardener in Florida. Minnesota may be preparing for frost while Arizona is still managing intense heat. Alaska and Hawaii have growing conditions unlike most of the continental United States.

So this almanac will not pretend one schedule fits everyone.
